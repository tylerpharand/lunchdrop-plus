# Privacy policy

Lunchdrop+ is a browser extension that adds quality-of-life improvements on [Lunchdrop](https://www.lunchdrop.com/).

## Data we collect

We do not collect user data. The extension has no account, no analytics, and no server of its own.

## Data stored on your device

The extension saves these settings in the browser’s local storage:

- The support amount you choose
- Whether extra prompts are hidden
- Whether long item descriptions wrap

Those settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

## Lunchdrop

On lunchdrop.com, the extension can set the support fee and hide extra prompts. The support change is sent to Lunchdrop as part of the order you are already placing there. We do not receive that data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/tylerpharand/lunchdrop-plus/issues>.
